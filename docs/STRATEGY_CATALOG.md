## Candlestick patterns

| pattern | bias | definition |
|---|---|---|
| Doji | neutral | Body <=10% of range: indecision. |
| Spinning Top | neutral | Small body (<=35%) with wicks both sides. |
| Hammer | bullish* | Small body, long lower wick (>=2x body); bullish after a down-move, neutral after an up-move. |
| Shooting Star | bearish* | Small body, long upper wick; bearish after an up-move. |
| Marubozu | bull/bear | Both wicks <=5% of range: strong one-direction candle. |
| Bullish Engulfing | bullish | Bullish body engulfs prior bearish body. |
| Bearish Engulfing | bearish | Bearish body engulfs prior bullish body. |
| Inside Bar | neutral | Range inside prior candle: compression. |
| Outside Bar | neutral | Range engulfs prior candle: expansion. |
| Morning Star | bullish | Three-candle reversal after a decline. |
| Evening Star | bearish | Three-candle reversal after a rise. |
| Three White Soldiers | bullish | Three consecutive strong bullish candles. |
| Three Black Crows | bearish | Three consecutive strong bearish candles. |
